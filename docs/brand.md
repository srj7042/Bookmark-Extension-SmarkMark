# Brand notes — the name "SmartMark"

## What a quick search found

Do not treat this as a clearance opinion — it is not legal advice, and trademark rights
are territorial and depend on use, not just registration.

| Mark | Office | Class(es) | Status | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SMARTMARK | USPTO | 9 (computer/electrical) | Dead, 2010 | Skyhawke Technologies — "continued use not filed", un-revivable |
| SMARTMARK | USPTO | 9 (semiconductor marking) | Dead | MCT Worldwide |
| SmartMark Communications | USPTO | 35, 38 (marketing/communications services) | Live | Same word mark, but services, not software |
| SMARTMARK | Rospatent (Russia) | 9 (software) | Registered, valid to 2030 | SOFTPROTRADING — territorial; matters only if you distribute there |

## What this means in practice

The name is **usable but not clean**. The dead USPTO class-9 marks mean "SmartMark" is not
currently registered as software in the US, but there is a live US word mark in adjacent
classes (35/38) and a live Russian software mark, so a US trademark application for class 9
software could still draw a citation or an opposition. Chrome Web Store items also must not
infringe trademarks or imply Google affiliation — so "SmartMark" as a standalone name is
fine, but **"Chrome SmartMark" or anything with "Google" in it is not**.

## Recommendation

1. Before you spend on branding, packaging or a domain: run a fresh search on IP India
   (classes 9 and 42), USPTO TESS and EUIPO TMview for "SmartMark".
2. Keep evidence of first use (dated screenshots, this repo's first commit) — in India and
   the US, rights can arise from use even without registration.
3. Domain: `smartmark.com` is very likely taken (`.com.au` was already subject to a
   registry dispute). Pick a different `.com` or a descriptive domain before printing
   anything.
4. Have a fallback name ready. Alternatives that keep the same idea: Markerly, Pagemark,
   Marklet, Foldermark, Markwise, Marqit.
5. Register the name as a trademark only once you have traction — first, establish that
   users actually search for it.

## Store-name rules (follow these whatever you call it)

- Name at most 45 characters (current manifest name is 28: `SmartMark – Smart Bookmark Manager`).
- No keyword stuffing in the name.
- Never imply affiliation with Google or Chrome: "Chrome", "Google" and the Chrome logo
  must not appear in the item name.
