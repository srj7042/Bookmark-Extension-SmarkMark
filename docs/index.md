# SmartMark — policies & launch docs

SmartMark is a local-first bookmark manager for Chrome. Everything it touches stays on
your device; the extension makes no network requests.

These pages are the published copies of the documents in `docs/` of the extension
repository:

- [Privacy Policy](privacy.html) — what SmartMark accesses, what never leaves your device,
  and your rights.
- [Terms of Use](terms.html) — the licence you are granted, no warranty, support, and
  governing law.
- [Security Policy](security.html) — how to report a vulnerability, what is and isn't in
  scope, and what the extension already does to stay safe.
- [Accessibility](accessibility.html) — WCAG 2.2 AA target, what is verified
  automatically (`npm run probe`/`npm run guard`), and known limitations.

If anything here contradicts what the extension actually does, that is a bug — report it
to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.
